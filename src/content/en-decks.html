假工作的终结

Strategy & knowledge work

Strategy & knowledge work/Public session/Chinese

Fake work swaps value that is hard to measure for actions that are easy to see. AI makes looking busy nearly free, so fake work will first explode and then lose its price. We could never tell real from fake because the visible layer of reports, plans and decks was costly to produce and served as a proxy for the skill below the waterline. That proxy has now failed, so we have to look directly at judgment and at what it grows from: a world model shaped by the scientific method, and craft shaped by practice. What most people lack is the ability, the habits and the mechanisms to see it.
